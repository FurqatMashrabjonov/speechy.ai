# Speechy AI — Track Strategy 2026

## Research Date: May 2026

---

## Why We're Doing This

Current 5 tracks are built around "presentation delivery" — the same space every competitor occupies (Yoodli, Orai, Speeko, VirtualSpeech). The white space nobody covers: **real two-way, emotionally charged conversations under pressure.**

Key differentiator we're building toward: AI characters that push back, get defensive, guilt-trip, and react like real humans — not polite interviewers who end every turn with a question.

## Competitive Gap Analysis

| Competitor | What They Do | What's Missing |
|------------|--------------|----------------|
| Yoodli | Sales pitches, presentations, "crucial conversations" (enterprise) | Coaches HOW you speak, not WHAT strategy you use; robotic AI that always ends with a question; no emotional pressure |
| Orai / Speeko | Voice quality, tone, gamified delivery drills | Monologue only; no real two-way conversation |
| VirtualSpeech | VR audiences, 40+ roleplay scenarios | Requires headset; B2B/L&D only |
| Praktika / Speak | AI conversation but for language learning (ESL fluency) | Not for native-speaker social/professional confidence |
| Tough Tongue AI | Difficult-conversation roleplay | Corporate L&D buyers only, not consumer |

**The one-sentence gap:** No consumer app teaches people how to hold a hard, two-way, emotionally-charged conversation in real time — they only teach you how to deliver a polished speech.

### TRACK 1 (REPLACE Well-Rounded): "Tough Conversations"
**Emotional hook:** *"I avoid the most important conversation because I'm scared of the reaction — and the avoidance is costing me money, respect, and peace of mind."*

**Why first:**
- Broadest addressable pain: workplace AND personal life, all ages, all demographics
- Negotiation training market = $2B, growing 7%; almost nothing for individual consumers
- Directly attacks the competitive white space no app covers
- Gen Z outsourcing hard conversations to ChatGPT (CNN, March 2026) — they know they need this

### TRACK 3 (FUTURE): "Real-World Social"
**Emotional hook:** *"I can text anyone — but in person I freeze, and I'm quietly lonely because of it."*

**Why third:**
- Loneliness epidemic: 2 in 10 US adults have no close friends outside family (vs 3% in 1990)
- APA 2025 poll: nation suffering from division and loneliness
- 44% increase in singles at offline events since 2025 (post-dating-app fatigue)
- Distinct from existing Social Confidence — addresses digital-to-IRL transition specifically
- Harder to monetize than career tracks (lower willingness to pay), so build after the first two

**15 Scenario Ideas:**
1. Keep a conversation going when your mind goes blank
2. Turn a dating-app match into a real first date without it being awkward
3. Walk into a party where you only know the host
4. Make a new friend as an adult — and actually follow up
5. Recover gracefully after you say something awkward
6. Join a group conversation that's already in progress
7. Small talk with a stranger (coffee shop, gym, flight)
8. Reconnect with someone you've lost touch with
9. Exit a conversation politely without it being weird
10. Be vulnerable with a friend instead of keeping it surface-level
11. Ask someone out in person (not over text)
12. Handle an awkward silence without panicking
13. Start a conversation with someone you find intimidating
14. Tell a story that people actually want to listen to
15. Navigate a big social gathering without burning out

---

### TRACK 4 (FUTURE): "Neurodivergent Communication"
**Emotional hook:** *"I'm exhausted from faking a way of communicating that was never built for my brain."*

**Market:** ADHD apps = $2.4B growing at 15.39% CAGR. No major speaking app serves neurodivergent adults. Zero competition. Prosper Health (autism/ND therapy) raised $16.2M in 2025.

**Needs careful design:** no time pressure in scenarios, explicit social rules spelled out, pacing controls, no harsh feedback tone. Ideally: clinical advisor input before launch.

## Updates to Existing Tracks

### Interview Prep — Add These 3 Scenarios
1. **AI Video Screening (HireVue style)** — answer questions alone on camera, no human present, timer visible
2. **Async One-Way Video Response** — record a 90-second answer, no do-overs
3. **5-Person Panel Interview** — multiple interviewers with different styles simultaneously
